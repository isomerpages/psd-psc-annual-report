<h1><strong>2024 PSC VISITS AND MEETINGS</strong></h1>
<p>The PSC values engagement with our partners and stakeholders. This allows
the PSC to keep abreast of governance matters both locally and overseas.</p>
<p>In July 2024, a delegation from the Public Service Commission Sarawak
visited the PSC to learn more about the Singapore Public Service. The meeting
exchanged views on the recruitment of talent and management of disciplinary
matters in the Public Service.</p>

</p>
<p>The PSC also makes visits to Ministries and agencies across different
sectors to better understand the work and challenges of the Public Service.
Through these visits, the PSC has the opportunity to interact with a wide
group of officers on the ground. This enhances the PSC’s insights into
the Public Service and enables the PSC to perform its roles and functions
effectively. The PSC would like to give its heartfelt thanks to all officers
for their hard work and dedication in building a better Singapore.</p>
