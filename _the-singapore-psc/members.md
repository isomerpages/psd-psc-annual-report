<h1><strong>MEMBERS OF THE PSC</strong></h1>
<p>The Constitution provides for the PSC to have a Chairman and at least
five but not more than 14 other Members. As of 31 December 2024, the PSC
comprised the Chairman, Deputy Chairman and nine other Members.</p>
